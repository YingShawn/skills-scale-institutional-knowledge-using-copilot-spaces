# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA and Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, test planning, and validation of acceptance criteria. They work with product and engineering teams to define testability requirements and ensure features meet quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Lead test execution and coordinate testing across teams
- Identify quality risks and blockers early
- Collaborate on Definition of Done to include testability requirements
- Report quality metrics and trends to PM and Project Manager

### Goals
- Catch defects early in the development cycle
- Ensure customer-facing quality and reliability
- Reduce post-release incidents through comprehensive testing

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and acceptance criteria review
- Test status in weekly syncs
- Quality metrics dashboards and release readiness reports

---

## Tech Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Tech Leads and Architects provide technical guidance, make high-level design decisions, and ensure solutions align with system architecture and scalability requirements. They mentor developers and mitigate technical risks.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical design reviews and architecture decisions
- Mentor developers on best practices and design patterns
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Ensure code quality, maintainability, and scalability
- Collaborate with product on technical feasibility and trade-offs

### Goals
- Deliver architecturally sound, maintainable solutions
- Minimize technical debt and long-term maintenance costs
- Build team capability and knowledge sharing

### Typical Communication
- Technical design docs and RFC reviews
- Architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Code reviews and mentoring feedback
- Technical risk discussions in planning and weekly syncs

---

## Sponsor / Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors and key Stakeholders provide executive oversight, ensure business alignment, allocate resources, and make high-level strategic decisions. They are primary recipients of project status and risk escalations.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and resource allocation
- Provide business context and strategic direction
- Make go/no-go decisions at key gates
- Receive and respond to escalations and blockers
- Communicate project outcomes to broader organization

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value
- Align project with organizational strategy
- Enable fast decision-making and unblock escalations

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and milestone reviews
- Go/no-go decision gates during initiation and planning
- Escalation notifications and decision logs
- Post-release retrospectives and outcome reviews

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate process execution, remove impediments, and help teams optimize velocity and collaboration. They ensure the team follows agreed-upon ceremonies and practices while continuously improving team dynamics.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove blockers and impediments to team productivity
- Coach the team on agile practices and ceremonies
- Track and optimize team velocity and capacity
- Help resolve conflicts and improve team collaboration

### Goals
- Enable consistent, sustainable team velocity
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement
- Reduce process friction and meeting overhead
- Build team autonomy and self-organization

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-one coaching and feedback
- Team retrospectives and action item tracking
- Metrics dashboards (velocity, burndown, cycle time)

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment activities, manage release schedules, ensure rollback readiness, and communicate release status to support and customers. They follow release checklists and incident playbooks.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release timing and deployment windows
- Verify pre-release requirements and readiness checklists
- Execute deployment procedures and post-deploy verification
- Prepare rollback plans and manage incident response
- Communicate release notes and status to stakeholders and support

### Goals
- Minimize deployment risk and downtime
- Ensure smooth, predictable releases
- Maintain visibility and coordination across deployment teams

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and pre-deployment checklists
- Deployment status and incident notifications
- Release notes and customer/stakeholder announcements
- Post-incident retrospectives
